About me

Hi, I’m Renu — a florist with a deep love for flowers, creativity, and the beauty of nature. I find inspiration in blooming gardens, delicate petals, vibrant colors, and the little moments in life that make us smile. What began as a passion for creating beautiful floral arrangements has blossomed into a way of expressing emotions through flowers. I carefully select every stem, combine colors with intention, and design each bouquet with love, turning simple blooms into something truly special. Whether it’s a birthday, a wedding, a heartfelt gift, or just a little something to brighten your day, I believe flowers have the power to make every moment more meaningful. My goal is to bring beauty, warmth, and happiness into people’s lives — one bouquet at a time. 🌷💐

My Services

Whether you’d like to visit my flower shop in person or order your bouquet online, this is the perfect place to find the arrangement that speaks to you. From classic roses to seasonal garden blooms, I create every piece with care, style, and a personal touch - perfect for any occasion.

Visit my boutique

Step inside my Chicago floral boutique and explore a world of color, fragrance, and inspiration. Here, you can see each bouquet up close, choose your favorite flowers, and even request a custom arrangement on the spot. I love meeting my clients in person and helping them create something truly special.

Order online
